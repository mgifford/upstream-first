# AI Contribution Policy

AI assistance is permitted in this project under the conditions below. The
policy applies to code, documentation, issues, pull requests, reviews,
translations, design artifacts, and community communication.

## Project rules

1. A human must initiate, review, and approve every external contribution.
2. A human must understand the submitted work and be able to explain, revise,
   and maintain it.
3. Material AI assistance must be disclosed in the pull request or issue.
4. AI must not be used to impersonate a person, fabricate experience, invent
   testing, or conceal uncertainty.
5. AI-generated reviews, comments, issues, or pull requests must not be posted
   automatically.
6. Private repository content, personal data, credentials, embargoed security
   information, and client-confidential material must not be sent to an
   unauthorized AI service.
7. Generated dependencies, APIs, citations, license claims, test results, and
   security claims must be independently verified.
8. Contributions must be small enough for meaningful human review.
9. The contributor must respond to maintainer feedback. Repeatedly generating
   new responses without understanding the discussion is not acceptable.
10. AI use does not reduce accessibility, security, privacy, provenance,
    licensing, testing, or sustainability obligations.

## Disclosure

For material assistance, include:

```text
AI assistance: [tool or model, if known]
Used for: [research, drafting, code, tests, review, translation, other]
Human verification: [what was reviewed and tested]
Known limitations: [none, or concise list]
```

Routine spelling correction or local autocomplete that does not materially
shape the contribution does not require disclosure.

## Prohibited use

- Mass-generating contributions across repositories.
- Claiming tests were run when they were not.
- Submitting work the contributor cannot explain.
- Generating or publishing security exploit details outside the approved
  private process.
- Guessing whether another contributor used AI based on style.
- Using an AI detector as evidence for acceptance, rejection, or moderation.
- Replacing required consultation with a simulated maintainer opinion.

## Receiving-project precedence

When contributing elsewhere, that project's policy overrides this one. A
receiving project may prohibit AI-generated code or other AI-assisted
activities. If its policy is ambiguous, ask before substantial work.

## Maintainer review

Review the contribution, not a presumed identity or writing style. Apply
ordinary correctness and quality requirements plus any disclosed AI-specific
risks. Request clarification when the contributor cannot explain a material
choice. Close or defer work that creates disproportionate review burden without
clear project value.
