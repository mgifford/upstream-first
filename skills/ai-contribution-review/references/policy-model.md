# AI Policy Model

## Find policy

Inspect repository and organization:

- AI contribution and bot policies;
- `AGENTS.md` and automation instructions;
- contribution, security, privacy, and license documents;
- issue and pull-request templates;
- recent maintainer decisions.

## Classify state

- **Prohibited:** stop AI use within the named scope.
- **Restricted:** remain inside defined activities, tools, data, or disclosure
  boundaries.
- **Permitted with disclosure:** use the specified wording and location.
- **Permitted under ordinary rules:** apply the same quality gates plus human
  accountability.
- **Unspecified:** do not invent permission or prohibition. Ask before
  substantial work.
- **Conflicting:** identify sources and ask the responsible maintainer.

## Classify activity

Assess separately:

- code;
- tests;
- documentation;
- issues and pull-request text;
- reviews and comments;
- translation;
- research and summarization;
- autocomplete;
- automated external actions.

An allowance for one activity does not authorize another.

## Precedence

Apply law, contract, confidentiality, repository policy, organization policy,
project guidance, then conservative defaults.

Do not infer AI use from style. Do not use detection tools to make acceptance or
moderation decisions.
