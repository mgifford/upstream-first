# Policy Discovery

Inspect:

- contribution and governance documentation;
- issue forms and pull-request templates;
- security and private-reporting policy;
- AI, bot, automation, and agent instructions;
- Code of Conduct and communication channels;
- license, CLA, DCO, sign-off, and provenance rules;
- supported versions, release branches, and compatibility policy;
- design-proposal or request-for-comments process;
- testing, style, documentation, and accessibility requirements;
- recent accepted and rejected contributions of the same type.

Record URLs or paths, branch or version, date checked, authority, short
paraphrase, and uncertainty.

Repository and organization policy override this skill. Ask before substantial
work when rules are absent or conflicting.

If the proposed contribution may disclose a vulnerability, stop this workflow
and use `responsible-security-disclosure`. Do not infer that ordinary issue or
pull-request instructions authorize public disclosure.
