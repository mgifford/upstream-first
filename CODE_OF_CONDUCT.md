# Code of Conduct

## Commitment

We will maintain a welcoming, safe, and equitable project. Participation is
open to people acting in good faith, regardless of disability, race, ethnicity,
caste, color, age, neurodiversity, sex, gender, gender identity or expression,
sexual orientation, language, religion, philosophy, national or social origin,
socio-economic position, education, or other status.

## Expected conduct

- Be honest, respectful, and concise.
- Respect different experience, communication, and access needs.
- Give and accept constructive feedback.
- Credit sources and contributors.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and repair harm where possible.
- Respect confidentiality and explicitly stated boundaries.

## Unacceptable conduct

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualization, or character attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Impersonation or misleading claims of identity, testing, expertise, or
  authorship.
- Repeated automated or AI-generated interaction that burdens contributors or
  maintainers.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Reporting and enforcement

Do not report conduct incidents publicly. Before community participation opens,
maintainers must publish a dedicated private reporting contact and name at
least two people able to receive reports.

Maintainers may warn, restrict, suspend, or ban participants based on severity,
pattern, safety, and the possibility of repair. Protect the privacy and safety
of affected people. Record enforcement decisions privately and disclose only
what is necessary.

This policy is informed by
[Contributor Covenant 3.0](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/3/0/code_of_conduct/)
but is not a verbatim copy.
